Privacy Policy for TopPilot

Last updated: September 06, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes our policies on the collection, use, and disclosure of information when you use TopPilot and its integrations with third-party platforms such as TikTok, YouTube, and Meta.

1. Information We Collect

TopPilot is an automated publishing tool operated directly by authorized creators. We only collect and access data necessary to authenticate publishing permissions (OAuth tokens) and basic profile identifiers as granted by the account owner.

2. Use of Data and Third-Party APIs

Data retrieved through TikTok APIs is used solely to verify account ownership and publish videos explicitly authorized by the account owner. We do not sell, transfer, or use user data for advertising purposes.

3. Data Retention and Security

Access tokens and publishing credentials are encrypted and stored securely within protected cloud vaults. Users can revoke access at any time through their platform security settings.

4. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at: brunorpmattos@gmail.com.
